# PACE 111T – Program and Career Exploration in Technology

## Course Overview

In this folder contains Victoria Raven's PACE 111T focuses on developing an academic and career plan through personal reflection, exploration of academic programs, and research into career opportunities within technology and related fields.

The course emphasizes:

- Identifying personal values, interests, and professional goals.
- Developing effective learning strategies and academic success habits.
- Exploring technology-related careers and educational pathways.
- Building professional networks and support systems.
- Strengthening communication, teamwork, professionalism, and integrity.
- Understanding UMGC's academic expectations, resources, and student support services.
- Exploring transfer credits and prior learning opportunities to accelerate degree completion.

## Course Units

1. **Reflect on Your Values and Goals** – Identify personal values, interests, and academic objectives.
2. **Learning How to Learn** – Explore effective learning strategies.
3. **Being a Successful Learner** – Develop habits and skills for academic success.
4. **Building a Successful Plan** – Create an academic and career development plan.
5. **Exploring Your Career Field** – Research career opportunities and professional pathways.
6. **Cultivating Your Network** – Develop professional connections.
7. **Engaging Your Support System** – Identify resources and support networks.
8. **Reflection: Putting It All Together** – Reflect on progress and finalize academic and career goals.

## Repository Organization

PACE 111T materials are organized alongside **Technical Writing** in a related subfolder because the two subjects complement one another and support UMGC's academic requirements.
